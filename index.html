Digital Shelf modules

Six modules across the Reckitt portfolio — Harpic, Lizol, Colin, Dettol, Mortein, Strepsils, Air Wick, Veet and Vanish. Consolidated from ten overlapping prototypes: each module now carries its sub-views on a strip at the top rather than as separate screens, and the alert engine opens as a drawer from any of them.

The modules

Executive Summary

The landing read. Choose the basis at the very top — share points or rupees — and every number below rewrites in that currency. Then the written summary, the revenue-at-risk ribbon, and the availability decomposition down to the purchase order and the pin code.

basis switch · AI summary · event counters · waterfall · activity trace · RCA tree · PO ledger · pin-code map · action queue Open →

Control Tower

Everything that turns a finding into a decision, in one place. Was five separate modules — Control Tower, Action & Value, Investment, Campaign and Pricing — now seven sections on one strip.

register · value recovered · trade-offs & guardrails · investment & campaigns · pricing & buy box · content compliance · what shipped Open →

Competition Sensor

The whole competitive read behind one strip. Was three places — two sensor builds plus the New Product Radar — now six sections against a competitor set you choose.

battlefields · index & growth · geography · reverse diagnosis · promo & price · new launches Open →

Availability & Supply

Weeks of cover and projected stockouts against when the next purchase order actually lands, fill rate by warehouse, and distribution against fair share.

exposure timeline · replenishment worklist · fill rate by DC · distribution · SKU explorer Open →

Promotions

Did the promotion we committed actually run — by SKU, by platform, by pin code. Compliance matrix, deviations and the event calendar.

committed vs actual · compliance matrix · deviations · locations · configurator Open →

Persona Insights

The same shelf read through whoever is looking — KAM, category or brand. Deliberately overlapping with the other modules: the point is that one dataset serves every role.

morning briefing · alert builder · tasks · replenishment · analytics Open →
