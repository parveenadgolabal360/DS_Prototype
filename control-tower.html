One pipeline, two stages

An action becomes an initiative the moment it goes live, so both tables below are the same object at a different point in its life. Proposed and in-flight work sits in the initiative register with its guardrails attached; completed work sits in the action register with what it actually returned. They used to be two screens, which is how the same intervention ended up counted twice.

Initiative register

Every intervention we are running, what it was meant to move, and what it cost somewhere else. A row is only green when both numbers agree — a target metric moving while its counter-metric breaks is not a win.

Action register

Baseline tracking at the grain the client asked for — how many are done, who owns them, and what each one has returned since its decision date.

Counter-metric watch

The guardrail set attached to each initiative at the decision date. These are chosen up front, not after the fact — that is the only way a breach means anything.

A breach does not automatically stop an initiative. Some trades are deliberate — the MRP correction on Flipkart gives up share on purpose to recover margin, and it is declared that way. What the register refuses to do is let a trade happen silently.
